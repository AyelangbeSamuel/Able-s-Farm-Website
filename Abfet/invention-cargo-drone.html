About This Invention

The ABFET Cargo Drone is a fully autonomous heavy-lift drone developed to optimize the delivery of agricultural goods, tools, and emergency kits across rural and urban landscapes. With AI-assisted navigation, solar-enhanced flight endurance, and real-time telemetry systems, this drone is designed for precision and efficiency in the field.
