The process

How to get your order

No home delivery confusion. Order online, get a QR code, walk in and collect.

📱

1. Create account

Sign up with your name, phone and email — takes 30 seconds.

🛒

2. Add to cart

Browse available birds, select quantity, pick your nearest depot.

💳

3. Place order

Confirm your details. We generate a unique signed QR code for your order.

📲

4. Get your QR code

Download or screenshot your QR code. It contains your order and depot info.

🏪

5. Collect at depot

Visit your chosen depot within 4–5 working days. Show the QR, our staff scans it.

Our QR system

Pickup, not guesswork

Each QR code is cryptographically signed with your order ID, product, and depot. Our workers use the Able's Farm scanner app to verify it on the spot — generic QR codes won't pass.

  • 🔐
    Cryptographically unique

    Every code contains a signed hash — cannot be forged or reused.

  • ⏳
    4–5 working day window

    Your code is valid for collection within the window shown on your order.

  • 📍
    Depot-linked

    Your code is tied to the depot you chose at checkout — collect there.

  • 📲
    Staff scanner app

    Our workers use a dedicated cross-platform app to scan and verify your code.
